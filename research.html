Research

My research centers on energy and environmental economics, with particular attention to household energy transition, rural clean and photovoltaic energy access, and the relationship between energy access, time allocation, and gender equality.

Research themes

Four connected themes organize my current research interests.

Energy & environmental economics

Applied economic questions at the intersection of energy use and environmental policy.

Household energy transition

Changes in the energy sources and technologies used by households.

Rural clean & photovoltaic energy access

Clean-energy access in rural communities, including photovoltaic projects.

Time allocation & gender equality

The relationship between cleaner energy access, household time use, and gender equality.
