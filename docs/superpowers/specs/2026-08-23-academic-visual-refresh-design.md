# Academic Visual Refresh Design

## Direction

The site will adopt a restrained academic profile style inspired by the direct personal introduction of HongAo Yang's homepage and the structured biography, education, and research presentation of Zhenxuan Wang's site.

## Typography

- Use only local system sans-serif fonts; remove all Google Fonts requests.
- Keep body text near 16px and limit major headings to a moderate 32–42px desktop range.
- Use weight, spacing, and thin rules—not decorative typefaces or dramatic size jumps—to establish hierarchy.

## Layout and color

- Use a plain warm-white background, dark neutral text, one muted academic blue accent, and light gray rules.
- Remove dotted textures, gradients, ornamental circles, large shadows, pill-heavy styling, and animated card movement.
- Preserve the circular portrait but use a simple border without decorative layers.
- Keep the homepage compact and content-led; inner pages use a narrow readable measure and consistent vertical rhythm.

## Components

- Header: compact name, role, navigation, and rectangular language button.
- Home: portrait beside a concise biography, affiliations, and text-forward academic links.
- About: compact introduction, simple education rows, and understated honors grid.
- Research: plain bibliographic list with year column, paper title, authors, and journal.
- Footer: single thin rule with email and profile links.

## Constraints and verification

Existing bilingual behavior, navigation, CV, Scholar, ORCID, email, portrait, and all eight publications remain unchanged. Automated checks will reject external font imports, serif display fonts, gradients, dot textures, excessive heading sizes, rounded card styling, and oversized desktop headings. Desktop and mobile browser checks will verify readability, no overflow, and working language/navigation behavior.
