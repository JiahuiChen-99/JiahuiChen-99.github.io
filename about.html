A little about me

I am a doctoral researcher in Energy and Climate Economics at Beijing Institute of Technology and a joint doctoral trainee at Duke University’s Sanford School of Public Policy. I use applied microeconometrics to study household energy transitions, climate-risk perceptions, and renewable-energy policy.
