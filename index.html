Welcome to my website! I am Jiahui Chen. I am a PhD candidate at the Center for Energy and Environmental Policy Research (CEEP), Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT) since 2021, under the supervision of Professor Hua Liao.

My research focuses on the economic and distributional impacts of energy transitions and climate change, particularly the role of clean energy access in promoting development and energy justice for vulnerable groups in developing countries, especially women and children.

I am on the job market in 2027.
