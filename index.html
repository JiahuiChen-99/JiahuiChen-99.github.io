What I work on

My research combines household-level evidence with applied microeconometrics to evaluate energy and climate policy.

Households & applied microeconometrics

Understanding how policy and risk shape household choices, opportunity, and well-being.

Rural clean-energy transition

Studying clean cooking, electrification, solar adoption, and the distributional consequences of transition.

Renewable-energy & climate policy

Evaluating policy design, climate-risk perception, adaptation, and inclusive low-carbon development.
